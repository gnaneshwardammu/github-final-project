
# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal attacks, and discriminatory language.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers, who will review reports and take appropriate action.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may issue a warning, temporary suspension, or permanent ban depending on the severity and frequency of the violation.
